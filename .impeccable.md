## Design Context

### Users
Takhayal serves small business owners and solo creators in Kuwait and the wider GCC who need professional marketing visuals without hiring a designer, learning Photoshop, or mastering prompt engineering. The primary job-to-be-done is to describe a visual need in Arabic and receive a ready-to-post asset within seconds. Typical users include cafe owners creating next-day offers, emerging fashion brands launching on Instagram, and real estate agents preparing listing graphics on short timelines.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel calm, precise, and cultural. Calm means restrained and quiet rather than noisy, pushy, or over-gamified. Precise means every surface has a clear purpose, with short labels and minimal feature clutter. Cultural means Arabic-first UX with local relevance, Gulf-specific visual cues, and a product that feels built in Kuwait rather than translated from a Silicon Valley template.

### Aesthetic Direction
Takhayal should feel like a quiet, well-lit studio: focused, modern, and confident through restraint. It should avoid cluttered creative-suite patterns, especially anything reminiscent of Canva's loud, option-heavy, Swiss Army knife interface. The experience should feel like a scalpel, not a toolbox: one input, one output, zero overwhelm. Arabic should be first-class in tone and structure, with local cultural references embedded naturally into the product experience.

### Design Principles
1. Reduce cognitive load aggressively: every page should help users move from intent to visual output with minimal decisions.
2. Keep interaction surfaces purposeful: each button, label, and control should have one obvious job.
3. Design Arabic-first, not translated-second: structure, tone, and examples should feel native to Kuwait and the GCC.
4. Prefer restraint over spectacle: spacious layouts, quiet hierarchy, and confident simplicity should win over novelty.
5. Make professional output feel immediate: the product should communicate speed and reliability without visual chaos.
